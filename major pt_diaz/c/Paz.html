Nico Paz

Nico Paz (Nicolás Paz Martínez) is an Argentine professional footballer born on September 8, 2004, in Tenerife, Spain. He plays as an attacking midfielder for Serie A club Como and the Argentina national team. Standing 1.85m tall, he is renowned for his exceptional left-foot technique, creativity, vision, and high-level spatial awareness.
