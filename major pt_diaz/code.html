Argentina's history spans from indigenous settlements and Spanish colonization to its independence in 1816, followed by waves of European immigration and economic prosperity in the late 19th century. The 20th and 21st centuries were marked by periods of political turmoil, the populist movement of Juan Perón, a brutal military dictatorship (1976–1983), and the subsequent return to democracy.
